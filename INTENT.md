# INTENT

Why this product exists and what each request is meant to achieve. Plans,
issues and reviews are judged against this file. Keep it about outcomes: no
implementation detail, no task lists. Update it with `/intent`.

> This is the template. Run `/intent` in Claude Code to replace the
> placeholders below with your product.

## Problem

_What is wrong or missing in the world today, and for whom? Two or three
sentences._

## Audience

_Who uses this, what are they trying to get done, and in what situation?_

## What success looks like

_How will we notice the product is working? Prefer things that can be observed
or measured._

## Principles

_The few trade-offs we have already decided, so we do not re-argue them on
every feature. Example: "private by default", "works on a phone first"._

## Non-goals

_What this product deliberately does not do._

## Current requests

_One entry per request in progress. `/intent` adds and updates them;
`/plan-to-beads` turns each into an epic._
